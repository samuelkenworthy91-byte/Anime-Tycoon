# Staff Progression — 999 cap

Staff career levels and raw Story / Art / Sound stats now cap at **999**.

- The original Level 1–12 XP thresholds are unchanged, preserving early-game and old-save balance.
- Level 13–999 uses a long-run mastery XP curve, so experienced employees continue progressing through the campaign and Dynasty Mode.
- Every level still grants +2 to the employee's main discipline and +1 to both off-disciplines, up to 999.
- Timed training and facility teaching can also raise raw craft beyond the old 99 cap.
- Mechanical output preserves the old 0–99 curve exactly. Stats above 99 use diminishing logarithmic returns, approaching an effective skill of about 240 at raw 999. This keeps mastery valuable without recreating the early snowball problem.
- Existing saves that banked XP while stuck at the old Level 12 ceiling receive their earned mastery levels and corresponding stat growth when the employee is normalised.
- Career titles continue beyond Living Legend with Grandmaster, Industry Icon, Auteur, Hall of Famer, Immortal, Mythic and Pinnacle milestones.
