# Ravi Shah — Research perk

`Rapid Prototyping` applies both of Ravi's research advantages:

- Research project duration: `×0.75` after Archive bonuses (25% less time).
- Research Data cost: `×0.90`, rounded to the nearest whole RD with a minimum cost of 1 RD (10% discount).

The research screen shows the discounted RD price while Ravi is the active showrunner, and the same discounted value is used for affordability checks, the actual deduction, research-job history, and the start notice.
