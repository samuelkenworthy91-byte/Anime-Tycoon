Approved expansion implemented on main: 297 arcs, 619 combinations. The original planning count omitted five net combinations registered at runtime (seven additions and two penalties moved out). They are now canonical and import order no longer changes the catalogue.

386 new combinations: 30 reusable structures, 274 IP recipes (two additional for each of 137 blueprints), 10 Second Chance Timeline recipes, and 72 complete-length structures in 24 families. See STORY_COMBINATION_CATALOG.csv for every exact ordered recipe and raw reward. Short/standard/extended/prestige limits remain 3/4/5/6. The player browser filters by length and offers pages of 24 alternative recipes.

## Rewards

New tiers: 2 beats +3 Q / 2.5% fans; 3 beats +5 / 3.5%; 4 beats +8 / 4.5%; 5 beats +10 / 5.5%; 6 beats +12 / 6.5%. Existing positive recipes receive tier floors. Longer family members and strict supersets suppress duplicate smaller rewards; independent structures contribute at 100%, 50%, 25%, etc., capped at 18 raw Q / 15% raw fans. Negative rewards and clashes remain active. Existing score multipliers (1.6 quality, 1.5 fans, 0.45 licensed weighting) remain.

Studies teach eligible blueprint unlocks, arc/genre fits including risks, structures and clashes. Owned IP rights or an already discovered studio blueprint permit IP research; successful 24/40 adaptations remain an alternative. Mastered targets grant newly eligible content when rights/franchise availability changes, without another paid study. Timed completion, migration and New Game+ retain study progress and notebooks. Mastery synchronization is cached by eligibility so daily play does not repeatedly scan completed fields.

## Auctions

Maximum three auction openings per 48-week industry year, counting automatic, player-commissioned, resolved and skipped auctions. Automatic candidates retain randomized chance/timing, with at least six weeks between scheduled openings. The player may commission once per year, counted within the three-room limit. Completing/skipping a room does not restore its annual quota. No new room opens over an unresolved room or prompt.
