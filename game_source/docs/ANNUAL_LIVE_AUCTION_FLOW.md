# Annual Live IP Auction Flow

- Auction opportunities are rare event rolls: at most one candidate can fire in each 48-week industry year, and some years have no auction.
- A fired opportunity pauses the live clock and opens the full-screen forecast alert.
- **Skip Auction** declines participation and resolves the room to AI. **Return to Game** hides the alert but keeps the auction available in Rights Market until the end of the current week. **Enter Auction** opens the theatrical stage.
- The stage deliberately reuses the awards visual language: stage background, red curtains, valance, gold presentation and poster focus.
- Four bid controls raise the current price by approximately **5% / 10% / 25% / 50%** (rounded to the economy's £5k increments, minimum £10k). AI studios counter instantly based on reputation, tier and genre fit. If no AI studio is willing to beat the player's offer, the hammer falls immediately.
- Each of the 100 user catalogue IPs owns exactly one hidden studio blueprint. A 24/40+ licensed adaptation discovers it. Once discovered, the arc is available to original productions and as an optional blueprint on other licensed adaptations.
- Every hidden arc strongly synergises with its source property's two canonical genres and has a discoverable combo with an existing standard arc; each also has a situational anti-fit so it is not a flat universal upgrade.
