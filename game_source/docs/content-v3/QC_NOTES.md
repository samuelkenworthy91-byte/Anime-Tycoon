# Final asset and content QC

All 227 runtime images were decoded, dimensions checked, and individually hashed. All 208 cast files have distinct hashes and exact canonical filenames. Divider-aware crops were inspected in four role contact sheets after the 26 generated sheets were visually reviewed. No sheet labels or black grid lines are intended in runtime crops. The 1774×887 source sheets yield roughly 438–440 pixel cells, resampled to 512×512; these are not natively generated 512-pixel portraits.

The first 32 cast portraits repeat an eight-face pattern and lean toward warm/olive tones. The user's instruction to diversify from that point was applied to subsequent human batches, varying light through deep skin tones, hair textures, ages and cultural bases. Those early images have been retained, as requested by the forward-looking instruction. Across later batches, consistent poses and costume motifs still repeat. The art is polished and coherent, but this handoff does not claim every portrait has a unique pose or perfect adherence to every prompt detail. Declared cultural backgrounds are authored character metadata, not inferred ethnicity from appearance.

Three new mascot species were aligned to their delivered paintings: 041 small tawny owl (original snow bunting), 043 mythic winged koi (original flying fish), 045 small woodland owl (original nightjar). The productionAdaptation fields disclose this; generation prompts remain historical records. Affinities, IDs and graph coverage were not changed. Pet 027 is a stylised robotic moth with a furry-looking face. Hidden affinities were excluded from prompts and epithets; genre cues can overlap semantically, so concealed-affinity behaviour still requires in-game review. Mecha-visible paintings use mechanical context; Samurai and Shinobi use the relevant sword/retainer and covert-action language.

Worker sprite/portrait pairs share one painting per identity. Eight pairs were reviewed on a dark background for edges, face matching and feet. The initial worker master had a rendered checkerboard rather than transparency and is excluded from runtime; the edited magenta source was chroma-extracted into real RGBA. Sprites are scale-normalised and portraits cropped from the same source. Three workstation assets were reviewed on dark backgrounds and have real alpha; workstation screens intentionally contain abstract software-like imagery, not player-facing text or interface controls.
